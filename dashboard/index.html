How the score works (transparent, rules-based)

Momentum 30% (24h price change) · Market health 25% (24h volume + liquidity) · NAV premium/discount 25% (DEX price vs Chainlink NAV reference) · Adoption 20% (total supply). Every component is shown in the brief JSON; nothing is a black box. Data: Coinbase Tokenized Stocks API (415 tokens, no key) + DexScreener live Aerodrome prices.

Methodology and full source: github.com/guosongyi427-creator/stocksage-base (repo link updated on publish).
